Grammar

Three structures from the lesson. Study each one, then complete the practice questions.

“THẾ NÀO CŨNG” / “THẾ NÀO… CŨNG”

Structures

Pattern A: Subject + thế nào cũng + verb
Pattern B: Thế nào + subject + cũng + verb

Meaning. These expressions indicate that something will definitely happen, or that the speaker is very confident about a prediction.

Original examples

  • Thế nào anh ấy cũng đi du lịch với gia đình.
    He will definitely travel with his family.
  • Trời thế nào cũng mưa.
    It is definitely going to rain.

From the dialogue

  • Thế nào chị cũng phải giữ phòng đẹp cho tôi nhé.
    You must definitely keep a nice room for me, okay?

How it works. The phrase “thế nào… cũng” signals strong expectation. The speaker is confident the event will happen, so it works like “will definitely” or “surely” in English.

Additional example

  • Ngày mai là sinh nhật Lan. Cô ấy thế nào cũng rất vui.
    Tomorrow is Lan's birthday. She will definitely be very happy.
Watch out: Do not confuse this structure with the question “như thế nào?”, which means “how?” or “what… like?”. In “thế nào… cũng”, there is no question. It is a statement of confidence.

“DÙ / TUY / MẶC DÙ… NHƯNG… VẪN”

Meaning. This structure expresses contrast or concession: something happens despite a contrasting situation.

Structures

Tuy A nhưng B vẫn…
Dù A nhưng B vẫn…
Mặc dù A nhưng B vẫn…

Alternative structures

B dù A.
B mặc dù A.

Original examples

  • Tuy tôi mệt nhưng (tôi) vẫn đi học.
    Although I am tired, I still go to school.
  • Dù trời mưa nhưng ông ấy vẫn ra ngoài gặp bạn.
    Even though it is raining, he still goes out to meet his friend.
  • Mặc dù trời mưa cô ấy vẫn đi pic nic.
    Even though it is raining, she still goes on a picnic.
  • Tôi vẫn đi học dù (tôi) mệt.
    I still go to school even though I am tired.
  • Cô ấy vẫn đi pic nic dù trời mưa.
    She still goes on a picnic even though it is raining.

From the dialogue

  • Tuy phòng đó không nhìn ra hồ được nhưng vẫn ngắm thành phố được, đẹp lắm.
    Although that room does not have a lake view, you can still enjoy the city view. It is very beautiful.

How it works

  • “tuy”, “dù”, and “mặc dù” introduce the contrasting situation.
  • “nhưng” introduces the contrasting result.
  • “vẫn” emphasises that the result stays true despite the situation.
  • In the alternative structure, the main clause can come first, followed by “dù” or “mặc dù”.
Note: Vietnamese speakers may omit “nhưng” in some sentences. Use the patterns above as the main teaching structures.

“CÀNG NGÀY CÀNG” / “NGÀY CÀNG”

Meaning. These expressions describe a gradual increase or change in degree or quality over time.

Structures

Subject + càng ngày càng + adjective
Subject + ngày càng + adjective

Original examples

  • Họ nói tiếng Việt ngày càng giống người Việt Nam.
    They speak Vietnamese more and more like Vietnamese people.
  • Cây hoa càng ngày càng lớn.
    The flower tree is getting bigger and bigger.

From the dialogue

  • Giá phòng ngày càng đắt phải không chị?
    Hotel room prices are getting more and more expensive, aren't they?

How it works. “Ngày càng” and “càng ngày càng” often indicate a continuing change. They commonly appear before adjectives or descriptive expressions. The sentence describes a trend, not a one-time state.

Simple examples

  • Thành phố ngày càng đông.
    The city is getting more and more crowded.
  • Tôi nói tiếng Việt càng ngày càng tốt.
    I speak Vietnamese better and better.

    Final Review

    A summary of the lesson. Revisit any section you want to practise again.

    Key vocabulary

    Key grammar

    • Thế nào cũng / thế nào… cũng: strong expectation or prediction (“will definitely”).
    • Tuy / dù / mặc dù… nhưng… vẫn: contrast, “although… still…”.
    • Ngày càng / càng ngày càng: gradual change, “more and more…”.

    Useful communication skills

    You should be able to:

    • Ask to book a hotel room.
    • Ask about room types and prices.
    • Ask whether breakfast is included.
    • Ask about room facilities and views.
    • Express a strong prediction.
    • Describe a gradual change.
    • Express contrast.
    • Take part in a conversation about hotels and travel.
